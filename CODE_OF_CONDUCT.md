# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We value the participation of every person and want all contributors to have an enjoyable and fulfilling experience. Accordingly, we are committed to a harassment-free project environment.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at jyothibaarawoot-byte (GitHub profile). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

For the full text, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
